Should you leave corporate to build your own company?

You already know how to succeed inside an organization. This quiz checks three other things: whether you'd be good at running your own venture, whether you'd actually enjoy it, and whether your situation makes now a sensible time to give up a corporate salary.

Would you be good at it?

  • Resilience to rejection
  • Self-direction without a boss
  • Selling and persuasion
  • Bias to action
  • Generalist range

Would you enjoy it?

  • Tolerance for uncertainty
  • Need for autonomy
  • Ownership vs. security motivation
  • Appetite for intensity

Plus nine questions about the leap itself: what's driving it, your industry edge and network, savings and fixed costs, household support, and how easily you could return to corporate.

Your answers stay private

This quiz is anonymous. Your answers never leave your device: nothing is sent to a server, and nobody, including the person who shared it with you, can see your responses or results. It's here simply to help if you're curious. Your progress is saved only in your own browser so you can pick up where you left off.

This is a structured self-reflection tool built on common founder research themes, not a clinically validated test. Answer for how you actually behave, not how you'd like to.
